# 🥛 Smart Milking - The Khata Book That Writes Itself

Welcome to **Smart Milking**! This is a simple, digital diary built specifically for dairy farmers in Bharat. It helps you track daily milk records, manage your animals, keep an honest *hisaab* (ledger) with your local customers, and track your payments — all right from your phone.

Best of all? **It works completely offline.**

🔗 **[Open the App Now](https://imasyyyyy5.github.io/Dairy/)**

---

## ✨ Why Smart Milking?

The paper diary was never built for this much math! Tracking daily yields across cows and buffaloes, managing different rates, and calculating past dues when someone pays late can get messy.

Smart Milking replaces the diary, but keeps the discipline. It's incredibly flexible and adapts to how *you* run your dairy:

*   **Adaptable Milk Pouring Methods:** Whether you charge a fixed rate per litre or use a Fat-Based rate that changes per shift, the app adjusts to your method.
*   **Daily AM & PM Shifts:** Log your morning and evening yields separately. If an animal isn't milking, just mark it, and the columns disappear.
*   **Auto-Repeating Customer Hisaab:** Set a customer's daily litres and rate once. The app logs it every day automatically and calculates their total due from their start date.
*   **Clear Expense & Payment Tracking:** Log feed costs, medicine, and payments received from milkmen or local customers. The ledger always shows exactly what is owed.
*   **Insights & Analytics:** View your total production, see which animals are producing the most, and track your net profit over time with simple graphs.

## 📲 How to Install

You can easily install **Smart Milking** on Android or use the web app directly.

### Android (Direct APK Download)
1. Download the [Smart Milking APK](smart-milking.apk) directly from the landing page or Profile tab.
2. Open the downloaded `smart-milking.apk` file on your phone and tap **Install**.

### Web / Progressive Web App (iOS / Desktop / Chrome)
1. Open the [Smart Milking App](https://imasyyyyy5.github.io/Dairy/) in your browser.
2. On Android/Chrome, tap the menu (⋮) -> **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
3. On iPhone/Safari, tap the **Share icon** -> **"Add to Home Screen"**.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

1.  **Set Up Your Profile:** When you first open the app, enter your Dairy name.
2.  **Add Your Animals:** Add your cows, buffaloes, or goats. Set their standard milk rates and whether they are currently milking.
3.  **Start a Record Period:** Go to the Home tab and tap "+ Record" to start a 10-day (or custom length) period.
4.  **Log Daily:** Tap on the active period to enter your AM and PM yields.
5.  **Add Customers:** Go to the Calc tab and manage your local customers. Set their daily milk needs, and let the app do the math!
6.  **Cloud Sync (Optional):** Go to your Profile tab to sign up for a free account. This will securely back up your records to the cloud so you never lose your data, even if you change phones.
