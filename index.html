why it exists

The paper diary was never built for this much math

Tracking daily yields across cows and buffaloes, then keeping an honest hisaab with every dairy and daily customer, gets harder every month a diary stays in use — torn pages, faded ink, and one missed entry throwing off the whole ledger.

Dairy Notebook replaces the diary, not the discipline. Set a customer's daily quantity and rate once, and it repeats on its own — with past dues calculated automatically from the day they started.

No app-store download, no heavy install — it runs straight from your browser and lives on your home screen.

what's inside

Built around how a dairy actually runs

Four working parts, each doing exactly one job.

🐄🐃🐐

Animal Profiles

Add each cow, buffalo, or goat once. Mark which ones give milk and AM/PM columns appear on their own — alongside vaccination and calving records.

📒

Auto-Repeating Hisaab

Set a customer's daily litres and rate once. It logs itself every day after that, and past dues are worked out automatically from their start date.

💸

Expenses & Payments

Log feed and medicine costs as they happen, and record every payment received — so the ledger always shows what's actually still owed.

📊

Dashboard, Graphs & Calc

See which animal earns the most, watch profit trends by month, and reach for the built-in calculator without ever leaving the app.
